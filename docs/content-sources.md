## Photovoltaic glass

- **Power Glass 2 Layer, COM-M1.** The marble-series datasheet gives variants of 250, 260, 270, 280 and 290 W, efficiencies from 13.0–15.1%, 1600 × 1200 × 6.9 mm, 30 kg, two 3.2 mm glass panes, POE/EVA encapsulation and a 10-year product / 25-year linear output warranty. The illustrated sales card conflicts with this output range. Use the mechanical and STC tables in the sheet. [COM-M1 technical sheet, pp. 1–2](https://solartechnologysg.com/datasheets/CdTe.pdf)
- **Power Glass 3 Layer, COM-CBS1-Blue.** The company’s blue product card gives 210 W and 10 mm. The attached family brochure lists sky-blue COM-CBS1 at 220 W and 11 mm. The photograph of blue translucent glass on the catalogue page also resembles the different COM-CA2 family depicted elsewhere in the brochure. We show the supplier's original catalogue photograph, identify the conflicting ratings and omit a planning wattage, confirmed dimensions, weight and warranty for this exact listing. [Product card](https://solartechnologysg.com/img/products/panels/cdte2back.png) · [Family brochure, pp. 2–3](https://solartechnologysg.com/datasheets/cdteprod.pdf)
- **Power Glass Hollow, COM-Z3-Tm.** The hollow-series sheet gives T20/T30/T40/T50 variant STC outputs of 210/180/160/130 W, 1600 × 1200 × 28 mm and 75 kg. A 12 mm air cavity and low-emissivity pane are documented. The `T` numbers are film removal ratios in this sheet. They must not be described as measured light transmittance. [COM-Z3-Tm sheet, pp. 1–2](https://solartechnologysg.com/datasheets/cdte3.pdf)

## Roof-integrated tiles

Pages 3 and 4 of the tile brochure give each CdTe tile's outer dimensions, active glass dimensions, typical weight, output and 25-year linear output warranty. Use the outer dimensions for comparisons. Roof coverage depends on overlap. The brochure also lists a second 65 W profile, COM-BC-65W. The company catalogue card identifies its published 65 W tile as COM-DJD-65W, so the showroom uses the dimensions and weight for that variant. Tile output warranty is not presented as a 25-year replacement warranty. [Tile brochure](https://solartechnologysg.com/datasheets/powertile.pdf) · [65 W catalogue card](https://solartechnologysg.com/img/products/panels/powertile65.png)

## Bifacial silicon modules

Only front-side STC output is used for nameplate capacity. Every silicon model has published external dimensions, mass, efficiency and output grades. Each carries a 15-year guarantee on product materials and workmanship and a 30-year linear output warranty in its individual sheet. Do not apply these warranties to the entire catalogue. The sheets distinguish manufacturer management-system certificates from product characteristics. Certification logos are omitted. Client names, owners, handover dates, measured yields, budgets and the Singapore company's contractual role are not supplied.
